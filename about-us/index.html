About Smaran Raut: Building Fast, Search-Friendly Websites for Nepali Businesses

Profile & Service Philosophy

Smaran Raut provides SEO-optimized website development, search improvements for existing websites, and ongoing website management for businesses in Itahari, Dharan, Biratnagar, and across Eastern Nepal. Our focus is on clean coding, fast mobile performance, and practical, measurable search visibility.

Clear, Practical Web Solutions Without Gimmicks

Many businesses struggle with websites that look good on a laptop but load slowly on mobile phones or fail to appear when local customers search on Google. We take a straightforward approach:

  • Clean, Lightweight Code: We avoid unnecessary plugins and heavy templates that slow down your website.
  • Search-Ready Structure: Every page is built with proper heading tags, clear titles, mobile responsiveness, and schema tags.
  • Local Geographic Focus: We help regional businesses in Sunsari and Morang get discovered by nearby customers searching for their exact services.
  • Ongoing Reliability: We don't disappear after launching your website. We provide continuous support, updates, and maintenance so your site stays secure and current.

Core Service Offerings

We organize our deliverables into clear, transparent options. You can review all of our structured packages on the Services Overview page, or explore our core practice areas below:

1. Fast, SEO-Optimized Website Development for Businesses in Nepal

Building new custom websites designed for fast mobile loading, clean markup, and natural search discovery.

2. SEO Health Checks & Ranking Fixes for Existing Websites

Auditing and fixing indexation errors, broken links, meta tags, and local search visibility for existing sites.

3. Ongoing Website Management & Search Visibility Growth

Month-to-month management, search console monitoring, new content publishing, and continuous improvements.

4. Website Speed Optimization & Ongoing Maintenance

Image compression, mobile bug fixes, security updates, and performance tuning to keep websites running fast.

Statutory Compliance & Professional Standards

All consultancy operations and web engineering deliverables strictly comply with Nepalese legal bodies and professional standards:

  • Office of the Company Registrar (OCR): Registered commercial entity governance in Nepal.
  • Inland Revenue Department (IRD): Tax transparency and invoicing under the PAN/VAT Act 2058.
  • Nepal Telecommunications Authority (NTA): Adherence to modern web encryption and digital security standards.
  • Advertising Board of Nepal (Vigyapan Board): Ethical digital marketing standards and consumer protection.
  • CAN Federation: Following ethical information technology practices in Nepal.
