Product Schema Implementation for Google Search & Shopping Snippets

Featured Snippet: Direct Code Implementation

Implement Product schema with nested Offer objects defining price, priceCurrency ('NPR' or 'USD'), itemCondition, availability ('InStock'), and aggregateRating to display star ratings and pricing directly in Google Search results.

1. Nested Offer Objects & Multi-Currency Support

2. Merchant Listing Experiences & Google Shopping Integration

Google Merchant listings require shippingDetails and hasMerchantReturnPolicy. Omitting these fields prevents product snippets from appearing in the free Google Shopping carousel.
