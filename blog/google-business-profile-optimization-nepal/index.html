How to Optimize Google Business Profile for #1 Ranking in Nepal

Quick Summary

To get the best results from your Google Business Profile in Nepal: choose the most accurate primary category, verify your physical address and phone number, upload clear photos of your workplace regularly, and ask satisfied customers for honest reviews.

1. Overcoming Profile Verification Roadblocks in Nepal

Historically, businesses in Eastern Nepal struggled with Google's postal mail verification postcards, which frequently failed to arrive via Nepal Post. Today, Google widely supports video verification and mobile SMS/OTP verification.

  • Prepare your physical signboard clearly displaying your business name as registered with the Office of the Company Registrar (OCR).
  • Have your Inland Revenue Department (IRD) PAN/VAT certificate handy during live verification if requested.
  • Record clear, uninterrupted video showing your street signage, walking through the entrance, and showing your team or counter.

2. Choosing the Right Primary Business Category

Selecting the wrong primary category can limit your visibility in the Itahari-Dharan area. Google places the highest weight on your main business category. Choose the category that describes your core service most specifically, and add secondary categories only for distinct additional services.

3. Gathering Steady, Genuine Customer Reviews

Search engines value a steady stream of authentic customer feedback. Businesses in Sunsari that receive regular, detailed reviews mentioning specific services (such as 'fast website design' or 'helpful consultation') consistently outrank competitors with stagnant, outdated profiles.

4. Regularly Posting Photos of Your Store or Office

Uploading real smartphone photos taken at your workplace helps Google confirm your physical presence in Itahari, while showing prospective customers that your business is active and open for visitors.
