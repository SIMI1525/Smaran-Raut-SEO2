Search Intent Classification: Informational, Commercial & Transactional

Featured Snippet: Direct Intent Blueprint

Match search intent by analyzing the top 3 ranking SERP results for format (guide, listicle, tool, comparison), addressing the core query within the first 60 words, and structuring subheadings to answer follow-up intent signals.

1. Deconstructing the SERP Intent Signatures

Before drafting a single paragraph, evaluate whether Google serves a featured snippet direct answer, a video carousel, an interactive calculator, or e-commerce merchant listings. Your content format must match this visual search intent.

2. The Position-Zero "Snippet Sandwich" Technique

Place an exact 40-to-60 word definition immediately beneath your primary H1 or H2 heading, followed by bulleted supporting lists or tables. This structure directly satisfies Google's answer extraction algorithm.
