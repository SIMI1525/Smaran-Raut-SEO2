SEO Services Explained: What Each Service Does and How It Helps Your Business

Quick Summary

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) helps your business appear when potential customers search for what you offer on Google. Here is what every service included in our packages actually means, written in plain language without unnecessary technical jargon.

1. Website SEO Setup: Building the Foundations for Google

When a website is first built, search engines don't automatically know what it is about. Website SEO Setup configures the technical tags, meta titles, descriptions, and site structure so Google can read and index your pages accurately.

This includes setting up Google Search Console, creating an XML sitemap, adding clean title tags for each page, and verifying that search engines are allowed to crawl your content without technical roadblocks.

2. Keyword Research: Finding the Words Customers Actually Type

Keyword Research is the process of identifying the exact search words and questions real customers type into Google when looking for products or services like yours.

For example, instead of guessing whether people search for "commercial lawyer" or "business legal advisor in Itahari," keyword research reveals the exact phrases, monthly search volume, and local intent. This ensures your website is written to match how your customers actually search.

3. Website Content Optimisation: Making Pages Clear to Visitors and Search Engines

Website Content Optimisation means improving the text, headings, and images on your web pages so that both human visitors and Google can instantly understand what you offer.

We organize your content into logical headings (H1, H2, H3), rewrite confusing descriptions into clear explanations, ensure your main services are prominently described, and add helpful contact links so readers can take action easily.

4. Website Speed Improvement: Making Your Website Load Instantly

When someone clicks your link on a smartphone in Nepal, they expect the page to open right away. If it takes more than 3 seconds, most visitors leave and click a competitor.

Website Speed Improvement involves compressing oversized images, cleaning up slow code, setting up smart browser caching, and removing unnecessary files so that your website loads smoothly and quickly, even on mobile data.

5. Ongoing SEO Services: Steady Monitoring and Ranking Growth

SEO is not a one-time switch; competitors update their sites and Google regularly updates its algorithms. Ongoing SEO Services provide continuous monthly care to protect and improve your visibility.

This includes monitoring your search impressions and clicks, checking for new search queries your customers are using, updating older pages, and fixing any new technical issues as your website grows.

6. Technical SEO Check: A Complete Health Diagnostic for Your Site

A Technical SEO Check is a thorough inspection of your website's hidden plumbing. Even if a website looks nice on the outside, underlying technical errors can prevent Google from showing it in search results.

We check for broken links (404 errors), incorrect redirect chains, mobile display issues, duplicate content tags (canonical tags), and security certificate warnings, giving you a clear list of what is holding your site back.

7. Search Performance Review: Reviewing Real Traffic and Click Data

A Search Performance Review analyzes the actual data from your live website over time using tools like Google Search Console.

We review which keywords are bringing people to your site, which pages get the most views, where visitors drop off, and how your positions on Google have changed. This gives you transparent, objective insight into what is working and where new opportunities exist.

8. SEO Updates: Refreshing and Updating an Existing Website

If your website was built a few years ago, its keywords, headings, and descriptions may no longer match how people search today.

SEO Updates refresh your existing site without having to redesign or rebuild the entire website. We update your meta tags, add fresh keywords to your service pages, and bring your technical structure in line with modern search guidelines.

9. Local Business SEO: Getting Found by Nearby Customers

If you run a business in Itahari, Dharan, Biratnagar, or surrounding areas, you want customers who are nearby to discover your phone number, address, and services when they search locally.

Local Business SEO focuses specifically on local searches like "dentist in Itahari" or "best hardware store near me." We ensure your business name, address, and phone number (NAP) are accurate, configure local Schema markup, and structure your website so nearby customers can easily call or visit your location.
