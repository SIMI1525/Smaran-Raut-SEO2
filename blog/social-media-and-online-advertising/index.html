Social Media and Online Advertising: How Ads, Google Business Profile & Google Maps Drive Customers

Quick Summary

Combining a fast, professional website with targeted social media advertising and a prominent Google Business Profile allows your business to reach both active shoppers on search engines and local customers browsing social platforms.

1. Facebook & Instagram Ads Management: Reaching People in Your City

Millions of people across Nepal use Facebook and Instagram every day. Facebook & Instagram Ads Management is the service of setting up, targeting, and running paid advertisements on these platforms to introduce your business to potential customers.

How it works

Instead of posting and hoping friends see it, paid social ads allow you to target specific audiences based on their location (for example, people living in Itahari, Dharan, or Sunsari), age group, and interests (like homeowners, car owners, or healthcare seekers).

How it helps your business

Targeted social ads generate immediate awareness for grand openings, seasonal discounts, or specialized services, directing interested viewers straight to your website or allowing them to contact you directly on WhatsApp.

2. Google Business Profile: Your Free Official Business Card on Google

A Google Business Profile (formerly known as Google My Business) is the official profile that appears on the right side of Google search results or at the top of local mobile search pages.

What setup and optimization involves

Setting up your profile correctly involves selecting the accurate primary business category, verifying your business address in Nepal, adding accurate opening hours, uploading clear photos of your storefront and team, and adding a direct link to your website.

Why it is essential for local businesses

When people search for services like "clinic near me" or "SEO specialist in Itahari," Google displays a 3-pack box of local business profiles with buttons to Call, Get Directions, and Visit Website. An optimized profile ensures you show up when ready-to-buy customers are searching nearby.

3. Google Maps Visibility Improvement: Guiding Foot Traffic to Your Door

Whenever someone searches on the Google Maps app on their smartphone, Google ranks businesses based on proximity, relevance, and local reputation.

What improves Google Maps rankings?

Google Maps visibility improves when your business information (Name, Address, Phone number) matches consistently across directories, when you collect genuine customer reviews and reply to them, and when your website has clear local address Schema markup connecting your domain to your map pin.

How it helps your business

Higher visibility on Google Maps turns nearby searchers into real walk-in foot traffic and direct phone inquiries. For regional retailers, clinics, cafes, and local service providers in Eastern Nepal, being the top result on Google Maps is one of the most reliable sources of daily inquiries.
