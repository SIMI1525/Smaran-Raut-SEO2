Client Work Examples: Practical Website Solutions & Search Growth

Overview of Practical Results

Here are real-world examples showing how our four core services—building SEO-optimized websites, improving existing websites, ongoing management, and technical speed maintenance—help Nepali businesses achieve reliable online results.

Medical Clinic · Itahari

Local Search Setup & Business Profile Verification

Initial Problem: A local healthcare clinic in Itahari had no verified Google Business Profile, causing patients to get lost and find outdated contact numbers online.

What We Did: Verified the clinic profile, fixed business hours and phone numbers, uploaded clean clinic photos, and added appointment booking links.

Outcome: Patients in Itahari and Dharan now consistently find accurate directions and services through our Local SEO & Google Business Profile Ranking in Itahari & Eastern Nepal strategy.

Hardware Supplier · Sunsari

SEO-Optimized Business Website Build

Initial Problem: The company previously relied only on Facebook, with no professional website to showcase their commercial catalog and building materials.

What We Did: Built a fast, mobile-friendly business website from scratch with clean product category pages, structured schema data, and clear inquiry forms.

Outcome: Contractors and builders across Koshi Province now send direct inquiries daily after launching our Fast, SEO-Optimized Website Development for Businesses in Nepal platform.

Professional Services · Biratnagar

Existing Website Audit & Technical Fixes

Initial Problem: An accounting firm's WordPress website had over 40 broken links, failed to load properly on smartphones, and had missing title tags.

What We Did: Cleaned up broken redirects, updated title tags and meta descriptions, compressed heavy banners, and fixed mobile viewport bugs.

Outcome: Pages now load under 1.5 seconds on mobile 4G networks, and the firm's key tax pages are properly indexed following our SEO Health Checks & Ranking Fixes for Existing Websites diagnosis.

Retailer · Dharan

Ongoing Website Maintenance & Content Updates

Initial Problem: A retail shop's website had not been updated for 18 months, leading to outdated product prices and software vulnerabilities.

What We Did: Enrolled the client in our ongoing management plan, updating seasonal product catalogs, performing monthly security backups, and monitoring Google Search Console.

Outcome: The website remains secure, up-to-date, and free of crawl errors month after month without any downtime under our Website Speed Optimization & Ongoing Maintenance plan.
