Cookie & Local Storage Policy

Cookie Usage Summary

We use essential technical cookies and browser local storage solely to remember user theme preferences and measure anonymous page speed metrics. We do not use intrusive third-party advertising cookies.

1. Essential Cookies

Essential cookies and storage items are strictly necessary for the website's basic functionality, such as handling responsive menus and calculator state.

2. Performance & Analytics

Anonymous performance data helps us identify slow-loading pages and improve mobile responsiveness. This information cannot be used to identify individual visitors.

3. Managing Cookies

You can modify your browser settings to decline cookies at any time. Our website does not require cookies to read our guides or submit inquiries.
