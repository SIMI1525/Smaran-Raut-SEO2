Our Core Website & SEO Services

Service Overview

We focus on four essential web solutions for businesses in Nepal: building custom SEO-optimized websites from scratch, improving search rankings for existing websites, managing websites month-to-month, and delivering reliable technical maintenance and speed improvements.

New Websites

1. SEO-Optimized Business Website Development

Custom, mobile-first websites designed from day one with search engines in mind. Built with clean code, sub-second load times, structured content, and simple navigation so your customers find what they need immediately.

  • Modern responsive layout for smartphones, tablets & desktops
  • Fast loading pages without unnecessary script bloat
  • Search engine meta tags, OpenGraph & Schema structured data

Local Visibility

2. Local SEO & Google Business Profile Optimization

Designed for local enterprises in Itahari, Dharan, and Biratnagar needing to capture nearby customer searches. We optimize your Google Business Profile, ensure accurate address listings, and build local search credibility.

  • Google Business Profile setup, category selection & verification support
  • Accurate Name, Address & Phone (NAP) citations across directories
  • Local business schema and city-targeted landing page structure
Learn About Local SEO →
Existing Websites

3. SEO Audits & Search Fixes for Existing Websites

A practical diagnostic checkup for established business websites that are not ranking as expected. We identify indexing issues, broken links, slow pages, and missing title tags, delivering a clear step-by-step fix list.

  • Google Search Console & indexation review
  • Mobile usability check and broken redirect cleanup
  • Clear, prioritized action plan for your developer or our team
Explore Website SEO Audits →
Support & Performance

4. Website Maintenance & Speed Optimization

Keep your existing website running smoothly, quickly, and securely. We compress large images, fix layout bugs on mobile devices, update outdated software, and resolve security warnings.

  • Image compression and cache configuration for faster load speeds
  • Mobile layout bug fixes and responsive touch target fixes
  • Routine backups, software patches and security health checks
Explore Maintenance & Speed →
Online Stores

5. E-Commerce SEO & Product Category Optimization

Help your online store products and category pages get discovered when shoppers search on Google. We set up structured Product pricing markup and optimize category navigation.

  • Schema.org Product & Offer data implementation
  • Optimized category breadcrumbs and product URL hierarchies
  • Clear product descriptions aligned with buyer search terms
Learn About E-Commerce SEO →
Ongoing Growth

6. Ongoing Website Management & Content Strategy

Continuous monthly support ensuring your website stays fresh, search rankings grow, and new customer inquiries keep coming in. We monitor rankings, add new service pages, and resolve emerging issues.

  • Monthly Google Search Console traffic monitoring and reporting
  • Content updates for new services, pricing, or business announcements
  • Ongoing internal linking and on-page search optimizations
