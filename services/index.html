Choosing the Right Package for Your Business

Every business is at a different stage. Here is a straightforward breakdown to help you determine which package fits your current situation:

Website & SEO Package

Best for businesses that want a fast, clean website and complete on-page SEO setup, but already manage their own domain or intend to handle website hosting and launch independently.

Complete Website Package

Best for businesses that want an all-in-one hands-off solution. We handle everything from design and SEO to registering your domain, configuring fast hosting, testing, launching your site, and ongoing maintenance.

Complete Business Growth Package

Best for retail shops, healthcare clinics, and regional services seeking maximum customer reach. In addition to a complete website and hosting, you receive managed social media advertising, Google Business Profile optimization, and local Google Maps visibility.

Website Re-SEO

Best for businesses that already have an active website and want to refresh their search engine rankings, update target keywords, and fix technical errors without redesigning or rebuilding the site.

Customisable Package

Best for businesses that need a specific combination of selected services. You pick the exact deliverables you need, and we structure a milestone agreement to fit.
