Finding and Fixing What Holds Your Website Back

Over time, websites often develop hidden issues—like broken links, pages that Google cannot read, slow-loading images, and duplicate pages. These problems prevent your most valuable services from ranking in search results. We run a comprehensive diagnostic check and give you clear, actionable fixes.

1. Helping Google Find and Index Your Pages

We verify whether search engines can find and index all your important service pages. If pages are accidentally blocked by wrong settings, broken sitemaps, or error codes, we resolve them so your content can appear in search results.

2. Removing Duplicate Pages and Confusing Links

When search engines encounter multiple versions of the same page, they may become confused about which one to rank. We clean up duplicate URLs and fix broken internal links so your primary service pages receive full ranking value.

3. Improving Mobile Performance and Usability

We test how your website performs on actual mobile phones, identifying heavy files, slow scripts, or awkward buttons that frustrate visitors and push your site down in search results. When site responsiveness issues stem from script bottlenecks and render delays, our companion Website Speed Optimization & Ongoing Maintenance service eliminates asset bloat to pass Core Web Vitals.

4. Clear, Plain-English Action Plan

Instead of handing you a confusing technical report, we explain issues in plain language and prioritize them: critical fixes first, followed by improvements that bring the highest return for your business. If an audit reveals that an outdated legacy CMS cannot be remediated economically, our Fast, SEO-Optimized Website Development for Businesses in Nepal provides a clean, modern replacement designed for superior search visibility. Alternatively, if your current site is technically sound and only needs refreshed keywords and content updates, explore our Website Re-SEO Package on the Services Overview landing page.
