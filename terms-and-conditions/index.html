Terms & Conditions of Service

Service Agreement Overview

These terms define our project scope, payment milestones, and professional commitments for custom website development, search engine optimization, and website maintenance services in Nepal.

1. Project Scope & Deliverables

All custom web development projects and SEO engagements begin with a written scope outlining agreed milestones, design deliverables, technical specifications, and timeline estimates.

2. Payment Structure & Invoicing

Our project payment process is structured into clear, transparent stages:

  • Stage 1: Advance Payment: An agreed advance amount is provided before development work begins to initiate the project.
  • Stage 2: Website Development Completion: Once the website is fully developed, tested, and completed, the client settles the remaining payment for website development.
  • Stage 3: SEO Service & Performance Review: SEO services are handled separately. After the website has been active for one month, we review its initial traffic and click activity together. When the website shows a reasonable level of traffic and engagement based on its actual performance, the client proceeds with the remaining payment for the SEO service.

All invoicing complies with applicable statutory guidelines under the Inland Revenue Department (IRD) of Nepal. Please note that organic search traffic develops over time; specific rankings, visitor numbers, or click volumes are never artificially guaranteed.

3. Ownership & Intellectual Property

Upon settlement of final project fees, full ownership of custom code, design assets, and content created specifically for your website transfers to you.

4. Website Maintenance & Ongoing Support

Website maintenance and updates cover routine checks, content additions, and performance adjustments as arranged with the client, keeping your site fast, secure, and user-friendly.
